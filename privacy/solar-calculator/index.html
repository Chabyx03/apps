Solar Calculator Privacy Policy

REILAB MARKETING · Effective 14 November 2026

Solar Calculator (Solar System Setup Calculator) sizes a solar power system from the appliances you list. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

The appliances, site settings and equipment you enter are saved in the app's private storage so they are there the next time you open it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. We have no copy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app works offline and has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect personal information, location, contacts or device identifiers, and it sends no data to us or to third parties. When you tap Copy summary, the summary goes only to your phone's clipboard.

Purchases

If you buy the app on Google Play, Google handles the payment under the Google Play terms and privacy policy. We receive only the sales information Google Play provides to developers.

Children

The app is a planning tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact REILAB MARKETING using the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
