Colony Counter Privacy Policy

REILAB MARKETING · Effective 14 November 2026

Colony Counter counts bacterial and fungal colonies on petri dish photos and calculates CFU/mL. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

Photos and the camera

The app uses your camera, or a picture you choose from your gallery, only to count colonies. Photos are analysed on your phone. They are never uploaded, sent to us or shared with anyone.

What is stored on your phone

  • Samples you save (name, picture, count and CFU result), kept in the app's private storage.
  • What the on-phone AI brain learns from plates you correct, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Marked pictures you choose to save to your Gallery, which then belong to your phone's Gallery.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything the app stored. We have no copy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect personal information, location, contacts or device identifiers, and it sends no data to us or to third parties. The app uses the internet only to load its display fonts.

Purchases

If you buy the app on Google Play, Google handles the payment under the Google Play terms and privacy policy. We receive only the sales information Google Play provides to developers.

Children

The app is a laboratory tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact REILAB MARKETING using the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
